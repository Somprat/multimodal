# Progress Report — 2026-08-08

## Completed: RGB vs RGB + Spatial Offline Probe

The frozen-feature probe was rerun using all 96 recorded SimplerEnv NPZ episodes:

- 4 tasks, 24 episodes per task
- 11,520 frames total
- Fixed split: 6,720 training, 1,920 validation, and 2,880 test frames
- Variants: RGB and RGB + point-cloud spatial encoder
- Seeds: 41, 42, and 43
- Training: 20 epochs per variant and seed

Mean test results across the three seeds:

| Metric | RGB | RGB + spatial | Better result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Object-position MAE | 2.60 cm | 3.05 cm | RGB |
| Target-position MAE | 3.95 cm | 3.72 cm | RGB + spatial |
| Object-distance MAE | 2.44 cm | 2.97 cm | RGB |
| Grasp balanced accuracy | 94.1% | 96.3% | RGB + spatial |
| Combined test loss | 0.322 | 0.215 | RGB + spatial |

Conclusion: spatial input improved target localization, grasp classification, and combined loss, but worsened object-position and distance errors. The evidence is promising but mixed. This was an offline diagnostic and did not measure closed-loop robot-task success.

## Completed: Full History vs Query Top-4

Both configurations ran the full robot policy over the same four SimplerEnv tasks with 24 episodes per task.

| Task | Full history | Query top-4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Put carrot on plate | 18/24 (75.0%) | 19/24 (79.2%) |
| Stack green cube on yellow cube | 9/24 (37.5%) | 8/24 (33.3%) |
| Put eggplant in basket | 24/24 (100.0%) | 24/24 (100.0%) |
| Put spoon on tablecloth | 18/24 (75.0%) | 19/24 (79.2%) |
| **Overall** | **69/96 (71.9%)** | **70/96 (72.9%)** |

Conclusion: query top-4 retained approximately the same success rate as full history while selecting only four memories. Its net improvement was one successful episode, which is too small and task-dependent to establish a reliable accuracy gain.

The detailed comparison is in [`query_vs_full_history.md`](query_vs_full_history.md).

## Paused: Spatial-Encoder-Only Policy Training

A 5,000-step pilot was investigated but deliberately not launched.

Reasons:

- The configured real Bridge RLDS training directory (`data/bridge-rlds`) is absent.
- The 96 SimplerEnv probe NPZs do not contain demonstration actions and cannot train the robot policy.
- The cached 100 real ManiSkill NPZs contain camera geometry only, without RGB or actions.
- The locally available RLDS fixture is synthetic and is suitable only for smoke testing.
- Spatial and spatial-fusion modules in the current policy checkpoint are untrained/randomly initialized.

No training process was left running, and no policy checkpoint was produced. A meaningful pilot requires first downloading or materializing a real RLDS action dataset containing RGB, depth, camera intrinsics, robot state, actions, and language instructions.
